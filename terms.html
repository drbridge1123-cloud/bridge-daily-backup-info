Purpose and authorized use

Bridge Daily Backup is a private utility for its operator's own backup and recovery needs. It is not offered as a public hosted backup service. Use it only with files, applications, and Google accounts you are authorized to administer.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for choosing backup sources, managing storage capacity and retention, protecting credentials, keeping recovery passwords separately, and checking backup results. A configured schedule alone does not establish that a backup completed successfully.

Restore tests should use a separate destination and must not overwrite live application data. Backup and recovery depend on the availability of the computer, storage, network, Google account, and recovery passwords.

Availability

No uninterrupted service or guaranteed recovery is promised. The utility is provided as is for private use. Maintain additional recovery copies when required by your needs.

Google account connection

Google Drive access is subject to the account owner's authorization and Google's applicable terms. The owner may revoke the connection at any time. See the Privacy Policy for data use and deletion information.
