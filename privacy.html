Bridge Daily Backup

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Google Drive access and data practices of Bridge Daily Backup, a private backup utility operated by the owner of the authorizing Google account.

Data accessed

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It accesses backup files and folders it creates or that are specifically authorized for it, including their contents and associated metadata. It may also read available Drive storage quota information to check whether an upload can fit.

The operator selects local application files, configuration, and database exports for backup. Backups may contain personal or confidential information from those applications. Backup contents are encrypted locally before they are uploaded to Google Drive.

How data is used

Google Drive access is used only to store, retrieve, verify, restore, and manage the retention of the operator's backups. OAuth access and refresh tokens are used to maintain the authorized connection for unattended backups.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Bridge Daily Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and access

Encrypted backup repositories are stored locally and in the operator's authorized Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's computer in a protected configuration. The utility does not send recovery passwords to this website or store them in the cloud backup repository.

Google Drive processes uploaded files and storage metadata as the selected storage provider. Files remain subject to the Google account's access and sharing settings. The utility does not automatically share backups with other people or make them public.

Retention and deletion

The operator controls backup retention. Removing authorization stops future authorized Drive access; it does not automatically delete existing backups. The operator can remove backups from local storage or Google Drive and separately manage Drive Trash.

Access can be revoked in the Google account's third-party connections settings. Local credentials can also be removed from the backup computer.

This informational website

These static pages do not contain a login form, collect backup files, or include application analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary website request information under its own privacy policy.
